# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Atrium to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. We pledge
to make taking part a harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Being respectful and kind, in reviews as much as anywhere.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback, about the work and not the person.
- Crediting other people's work.
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing anyone's health data without their consent.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and any event run by the project) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact on [localforge.in](https://localforge.in) or a
private message on GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions,
or ban contributors who break this code, temporarily or permanently.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
